Three steps, no coding

Everything runs in the browser. You don't need to install any program, and you can try JIANT before touching your FM-1.

01

Try it in the browser

The emulator runs the real firmware on your computer or phone. Play with the keyboard, load a song, hear every engine.

  • No FM-1 needed
  • Renders the stems of a song from your backup
Open the emulator
02

Install it on your FM-1

Open the installer in Chrome or Edge, connect the FM-1 with a USB data cable, press Install and wait without unplugging.

  • The installer checks your FM-1 first and saves a backup
  • Coming from another custom firmware? Return to official V15 first, with the installer's button
Open the installer
03

Edit and back up

The web editor shows your sounds and settings on the computer screen while the FM-1 keeps playing.

  • Send DX7 banks to FM6, manage presets
  • Back up every project with one click
Open the editor

Needs: Chrome or Edge on a computer (WebUSB and Web MIDI) · a USB cable that carries data, not only charge.

What's inside

One organism, eight species

Each engine is a species, drawn by the firmware itself. On HOME every track is a creature of its engine's species: its shape comes from its knobs, and it swells and heats up with its own audio.

The eight engines, as the FM-1 draws them

Aengines

8 synthesis engines

ANALOG, FM6 (six operators, 32 algorithms, DX7 banks), PHASE, LOFI, VOICE, WHEEL, NOISE and DRUM-X. No samples: everything is synthesized.

Bheart

DRUM-X

A drum kit of eight synthesized sounds, each with two sides. The MORPH knob moves the whole kit from one to the other, live.

Cnervous system

64-step sequencer

A piano roll per track, chords, probability, ratchets, extra playheads, and MUTATE, which transforms sound and pattern every few bars.

Dmembrane

Effects and master

Delay, five reverbs (SHIMMER, RESO, CLOUD…), chorus, slicer, live punch-in effects, and a master with limiter, clip and punch.

Ememory

Songs and the generator

Four sections per project, an ARRANGE page to chain them, and a generator that writes a whole song idea from a genre.

Froots

USB audio and MIDI

The FM-1 becomes an audio interface: every track on its own channel into your DAW (STEMS). MIDI over USB, Bluetooth and TRS.

FAQ

Before you install

Can I go back to the official firmware?

Yes. The installer has a button to return to the official V15 firmware (you choose the official FM-1.fwsc from M-VAVE). It saves a complete backup first.

Which browser do I need?

Chrome or Edge on a computer, for the installer and the editor: they talk to the FM-1 over USB. The emulator also works in other browsers and on phones.

Will I lose my sounds or projects?

Back them up with the editor before installing or changing version. The installer can also install earlier versions, but a project saved by a newer version may not open right in an older one.

The installer doesn't find my FM-1.

Use a USB cable that carries data (many cables only charge), connect it straight to the computer without a hub, and close other programs that use the FM-1 over MIDI.
