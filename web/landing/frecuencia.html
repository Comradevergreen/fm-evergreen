Frequencyin nature

An atlas of things that come back: from the Sun’s slow cycle to the light inside a leaf, measured in octaves of A, 440 Hz.

climb the ladder ↓
epigraph

Everything vibrates. Matter is light, light is frequency, colour is frequency. A percussion polyrhythm, sped up, becomes a chord; a solar cycle is, at some point, an LFO.

This synth and this system are about that: how frequencies relate in nature, from the slowest to the fastest, and how much of it a synthesizer already uses. The unit is the octave: twice as fast. The reference is A, 440 Hz.

Plate Ithe ladder

One ladder, almost a hundred octaves

Each rung is an octave: twice as fast. A 440 Hz is rung zero. A day sits 25 octaves below it; visible light about 40 above, and it fills exactly one octave. Drag, zoom, tap a point.

Sound is moving air; light is an electromagnetic field. They are different things, but cycles per second are counted the same way, and doubling them is always an octave.

Plate IIrhythm → pitch

A rhythm, sped up, is a chord

Three pulses in the ratio 4:5:6. Slow, it is a polyrhythm: you can count it. Fast, you can’t count it anymore and the ear fuses it into pitch: a major triad. Between the two there is no border, only speed. DRUM-X’s polyrhythms and the sequencer’s ratchets are chords played very slowly.

Plate IIILFO → FM

Two frequencies talking

One oscillator moves another. Slowly, it is an LFO: tremolo, vibrato, a breath. Push the rate past about 20 Hz and the movement stops sounding like movement: new frequencies appear on both sides of the tone (sidebands) and the timbre changes. That is FM synthesis. JIANT’s FM6 engine is six oscillators talking like this.

Plate IVnote → colour

The colour of a note

Double 440 Hz forty times and you reach 484 THz: light of 620 nm, orange-red. Visible light spans exactly one octave (400–800 THz), so every note has one colour, and only one. It is a game of octaves, not physics: a note does not glow. But the ratio is the same: an octave is a doubling, in air and in light.

Look how the octave closes like a colour wheel: violet sits next to red.

Plate Vday → LFO

A day is a very slow LFO

The sun rises and sets once every 24 h: 11.6 µHz. Speed it up octave by octave. At 16 octaves it is a slow LFO (0.76 Hz) breathing on a drone; at 25 it sounds by itself (388 Hz, close to a G). The shape stays the same; only the scale changes. The Sun’s 11-year cycle, 32 octaves up, is a 12 Hz LFO.

Plate VIin JIANT FM

The same gestures, inside the FM-1

FM6 · pollen

FM synthesis

Six operators, 32 algorithms: each one an oscillator that moves the frequency of another. What plate III does with two, FM6 does with six, and it reads DX7 banks.

DRUM-X · colonia

Rhythm on the edge of pitch

A drum kit on the edge of pitch: polyrhythms, ratchets and X-MOD’s tempo-locked LFO. Play a roll fast enough and it stops being a roll: it becomes a tone.

ANALOG · radiolaria

Microscope, thermal camera, spectrum

The firmware looks at sound the way we look at nature: through a microscope (the creatures on HOME), with a thermal camera (their heat ramp, the palette of the infrared your body emits) and as a spectrum.
